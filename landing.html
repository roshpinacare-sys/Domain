The honesty laws (non-negotiable)

  • Measured, never declared. Every number on this site is derived from a public source at bake time — with the source link printed under it.
  • Failure is displayed. A dead source shows "not measured this edition" — never a fabricated value.
  • Zero secrets in public paths. Everything you can see is keyless; secrets never touch a public artifact.
  • Zero fragile scripts. The public pages are static HTML — nothing that can die at parse time in your browser.
