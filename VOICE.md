# פרק-הקול · The Voice Charter

This is the law document for every public word of the estate: pages, posts, decks, receipts, commit subjects. It is enforced by measurement, not by memory — `agents/voice-gate.mjs` scans the live site and publishes `truth/voice.json`. The gate is the enforcer; this file is the law.

## 1 · קול ריבוני · sovereign voice

The network speaks as itself: a machine that measures machines, in first person plural where "we" means the estate, never a narrator outside it. The voice is direct, dry, and specific. It does not perform excitement and does not apologize for numbers.

## 2 · אין פרסונת-בעלים בטקסט ציבורי · no owner-persona in public copy

Public copy carries no directives, requests, or quoted speech of a person. The phrases "הוראת המפעיל", "המפעיל שאל", "בהחלטת בעלים", "במילים של הבעלים", "the owner asked", "owner decision", "owner action" and their inflections do not appear in public text. A real dependency on a human gate stays — written as mechanism: "release gate", "שער-הפצה", "the signing gate". The dependency remains; the person disappears.

## 3 · אין מילים-סימן · no AI tells

- Straight quotes only: U+0022 and U+0027. Typographic marks (U+201C, U+201D, U+2018, U+2019) are law violations, visible or injected from JS string templates.
- At most one colon per heading.
- Numbers with dates over adjectives: "32,603 tx/s · נמדד 2026-09-07", never "בלתי-נתפס בביצועים".
- No emoji in instruments (pages, receipts, decks, reports).
- Banned vocabulary, EN: delve, robust, seamless, leverage, unlock, empower, journey, elevate, craft, comprehensive, game-changing, revolutionary, cutting-edge. HE: מהפכני, חדשנות, חדשני, פורץ-דרך, אולטימטיבי, מדהים, מושלם, אדיר, עוצמתי.
- The negated form is the anti-hype and stays legal: "לא-מושלם" is honesty, not hype.

## 4 · טענה = מספר + תאריך + קבלה · claims with receipts

Every measurable claim carries its number, its measurement date, and a link a stranger can open. A number that cannot be evidenced is written as "אין-נתון" — the honest absence beats an invented figure. A stale number is either refreshed or dated; it is never silently reused.

## 5 · מילון שמות קנוני · canonical naming

One name per thing, everywhere: SAOS-NET (the state machine), Sovereign Runner-Kit (the on-device measurement kit), JUMPPER (the batch proof notary), שער-האמת (the Truth Gate, `truth.html`), דלת-הנזילות (the deposit cycle, `deposits.html`), Forge (the on-device executor and scheduler). A product is never renamed inside a sentence. A retired surface is marked retired, not relinked.

## 6 · עברית שוטפת · fluent Hebrew

No kebab-sentences: Hebrew is not written as endless hyphenated chains — hyphens serve grammar (שער-האמת), not typography. No invented words, no translated-English syntax where natural Hebrew exists. If a sentence reads like a machine translated it, the sentence is wrong, not the reader.

## Enforcement

- `agents/voice-gate.mjs`: personal-directive patterns and typographic marks are law (a hit fails the run, in public). Banned vocabulary is reported as editorial findings — word choice is judgment, marks are law.
- The dated archive keeps the voice of its day: history is not rewritten.
- Every page that breaks a law here is either fixed or honestly listed as a residual in the round's worklog. Silent drift is the only forbidden state.
